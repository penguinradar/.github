## What it does

- **View.** Watch public stories anonymously, see who liked a post, browse a follower list and export it to CSV.
- **Track.** Follow an account over time and get each new follow, new follower and unfollow with the date it was detected.
- **Understand.** Find mutual follows and who doesn't follow back. On paid plans, ask the AI Analyst what changed.

No Instagram password. No ads. Public accounts only. Free to start.

## Good to know

- Times are when a change was detected, not when it happened on Instagram. Instagram doesn't share follow times.
- Account owners can remove their account for free at [penguinradar.com/removal](https://penguinradar.com/removal).
- PenguinRadar is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
